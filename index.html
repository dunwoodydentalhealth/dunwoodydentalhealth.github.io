About Dunwoody Dental Health

Dunwoody Dental Health is a trusted dental practice dedicated to helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles through personalized and compassionate care. Our experienced team provides comprehensive dental services for children, adults, and seniors in a comfortable and welcoming environment.

Schedule Your Visit

Personalized, Compassionate Dental Care

We combine modern dental technology with a patient first approach to deliver high quality treatment tailored to individual needs. From routine cleanings and preventive care to cosmetic dentistry, dental implants, restorative treatments, and emergency dental services, we focus on long term oral health and patient comfort.

We believe every patient deserves honest communication, gentle care, and treatment plans designed around their goals. Whether you are visiting for a regular checkup or a more advanced procedure, our team is committed to making every appointment a positive experience while helping you maintain a healthy smile for years to come.

Our Services

Preventive Dentistry

Regular exams, professional cleanings, digital X rays, and preventive treatments help protect your teeth, detect problems early, and maintain excellent oral health for every stage of life.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Enhance your smile with teeth whitening, veneers, bonding, and other cosmetic treatments that improve the appearance of your teeth while maintaining a natural look.

Dental Implants

Replace missing teeth with durable dental implants that restore function, improve confidence, and provide a long lasting solution for a complete and healthy smile.

Restorative Dentistry

Repair damaged or missing teeth with crowns, bridges, fillings, and dentures designed to restore strength, comfort, and normal chewing ability.

Emergency Dentistry

Receive prompt dental care for toothaches, broken teeth, infections, and other urgent dental concerns to relieve pain and prevent further complications.

Clear Aligners

Straighten your teeth with clear aligners that offer a comfortable, discreet, and convenient alternative to traditional braces for many patients.

Family Dentistry

Comprehensive dental care for children, teenagers, adults, and seniors with personalized treatments that support healthy smiles throughout every stage of life.

Gum Disease Treatment

Protect your gums with professional periodontal care that helps reduce infection, improve gum health, and support the long term stability of your teeth.
