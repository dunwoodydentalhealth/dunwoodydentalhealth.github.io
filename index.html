About Dunwoody Dental Health

Dunwoody Dental Health is a trusted dental practice dedicated to helping patients achieve healthy, confident smiles through personalized and compassionate care. Our experienced team provides comprehensive dental services for children, adults, and seniors in a comfortable and welcoming environment.

We combine modern dental technology with a patient first approach to deliver high quality treatment tailored to individual needs. From routine cleanings and preventive care to cosmetic dentistry, dental implants, restorative treatments, and emergency dental services, we focus on long term oral health and patient comfort.

Our Services

Preventive Dentistry

Regular exams, professional cleanings, digital X rays, and preventive treatments help protect your teeth and maintain excellent oral health.

Cosmetic Dentistry

Enhance your smile with teeth whitening, veneers, bonding, and other cosmetic treatments that improve appearance while maintaining a natural look.

Dental Implants

Replace missing teeth with durable dental implants that restore function, improve confidence, and provide a long lasting solution.

Restorative Dentistry

Repair damaged or missing teeth with crowns, bridges, fillings, and dentures designed to restore strength and comfort.

Emergency Dentistry

Receive prompt dental care for toothaches, broken teeth, infections, and other urgent dental concerns to relieve pain.

Clear Aligners

Straighten your teeth with clear aligners that offer a comfortable, discreet alternative to traditional braces.

Family Dentistry

Comprehensive dental care for children, teenagers, adults, and seniors with personalized treatments for every stage of life.

Gum Disease Treatment

Professional periodontal care that helps reduce infection, improve gum health, and support long term stability of your teeth.
